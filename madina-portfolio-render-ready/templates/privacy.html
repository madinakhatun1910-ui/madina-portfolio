Privacy

Your enquiry information

When you submit the contact form, the website stores the enquiry fields you provide — name, phone, email, product of interest and message — in the site's local SQLite database so Madina can respond.

The site does not store product catalogue data in the database. Product information remains in data/products.json, and blog content remains in data/blogs.json.

Keep the site and its database secured if you deploy it publicly, and configure appropriate hosting, backups and access controls for any personal information you receive.
